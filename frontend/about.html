Privacy Policy and GDPR Compliance

Our site is designed to not use any cookies or trackers! The site is scripted with a third-party GDPR site-counter from Simple Analytics where only site visits are tracked as purely anonymous users. We do not store or keep any user-related data.

Additionally, there is an image cache to speed up the loading of images for this site. No user information is stored in this cache, only public accessable images for the adoptable animals!
